Cybersecurity Problems NexaVault Solves

Zero-Trust Architecture
🔐

Encrypted Storage at Rest

Medical reports are encrypted with unique per-file 256-bit keys before touching disk. Plaintext reports never persist unencrypted.

👨‍⚕️

Strict Doctor-Patient Scoping

Doctors can ONLY view records of assigned patients or patients who explicitly consented to a time-limited sharing grant.

✋

Patient Consent Sovereignty

Patients have complete visibility into their documents, approve or decline sharing requests, and can revoke grants at any moment.

🚫

Clinical Separation of Duty

Finance and desk staff are restricted from clinical data. Attempted access to clinical endpoints is blocked with HTTP 403 and logged.

🛡️

Cryptographic Integrity Check

AES-GCM authentication tags and SHA-256 baselines verify files prior to streaming. Any bit-flip tampering raises immediate Critical alarms.

📊

Tamper-Evident Forensics

Every access, upload, grant, and authentication attempt is chained with SHA-256 hashes. Audit logs cannot be secretly altered or deleted.
